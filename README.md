# UCLA MFE Math Bootcamp (Summer 2026)

Welcome to the official repository for the Summer 2026 UCLA MFE Math Bootcamp. This course is designed to prepare incoming Master of Financial Engineering students for the rigors of the UCLA MFE program.

### Course Objectives

This bootcamp is taught at an upper-division undergraduate level. It aims to bridge the gap between pure mathematical theory and the practical application of quantitative finance models.

### Grading and Assessment

The course is graded on a **Pass/No Pass (P/NP)** basis. The grading criteria are as follows:

* **Quizzes:** There will be six quizzes posted on BruinLearn.


* **Policy:** You may drop your lowest quiz grade.


* **Weighting:** Each remaining quiz is worth 20% of your final grade.


* **Passing Requirement:** You must maintain an average score of at least 65% on the quizzes to pass the course.

### Reference Materials

Students are not required to purchase these books, but they serve as the foundational references for this course:

* **Calculus:** James Stewart, *Calculus*, 3rd ed., 1995.
* **Analysis:** Walter Rudin, *Principles of Mathematical Analysis*, 1976.
* **Real Analysis:** Charles Pugh, *Real Mathematical Analysis*, 2002.
* **Linear Algebra:** Serge Lang, *Linear Algebra*, 3rd ed., 1987; Steven Roman, *Advanced Linear Algebra*, 2nd ed., 2005.
* **Probability/Stats:** Morris DeGroot and Mark Schervish, *Probability and Statistics*, 4th ed., 2013.
* **Machine Learning:** Marcos Lopez de Prado, *Machine Learning for Asset Managers*, 2020.
* **Stochastic Calculus:** Martin Haugh, *[A Brief Introduction to Stochastic Calculus](https://www.columbia.edu/~mh2078/FoundationsFE/IntroStochCalc.pdf)*, 2016.
